**How AI Helps CFOs Improve FP&A** 

AI can enhance day-to-day planning through automation and the early identification of key trends in finance.

**Faster Forecasting**: AI can utilise historical and real-time data to enable faster forecasting and planning.

**Improved Scenario Planning**: CFOs can run different scenarios of revenues, expenses, or growth before undertaking any large-scale decisions.

**Less Manual Reporting**: AI can automate data processing to minimise manual data work.

**Earlier Risk Identification**: AI can provide early warnings about strange trends, rising costs, or performance gaps.

**Better Business Insights**: Finance leaders can provide valuable insights about financial data to operating teams.

**Additional Time for Strategic Planning**: Thanks to the automation of analytical processes, FP&A can spend more time advising the business and growing the company.

**Why AI Makes FP&A More Valuable to the Business**

The true value is not just the improved reporting speed. It is about providing CFOs with a better understanding of what might happen next and what needs to be done.

**Leverages More Data**: AI enables CFOs to leverage financial and operational data for a holistic view of the business.

**Accelerates Decision-Making**: People get access to valuable insights without having to wait for long reporting processes.

**Facilitates Proactive Planning**: CFOs can detect emerging trends and adjust plans in advance.

**Improves Collaboration**: Finance provides business people with actionable insights promptly.

**Scalability**: Automated processes enable finance teams to deal with growing amounts of data without extra work.

**Enables Modern Finance Function**: AI enables CFOs to move from backward-looking reporting to forward-looking decision-making.

**The Bottom Line**
In the case of CFOs, the value proposition of AI in FP&A can be stated quite simply: better decisions in less time. AI can cut down on inefficiencies such as manual tasks, create improved visibility, and drive smarter planning processes. That is how finance can become a more strategic partners.
